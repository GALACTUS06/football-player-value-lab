# Recording script

1. Open the page and point out the dataset unit, record count, title, and default market-value distribution.
2. Select `position` and show the categorical bar chart with category labels and count axis.
3. Select `market_value` and show the equal-width histogram.
4. Switch between Upright and Sideways and explain the updated axes.
5. Click `Show scatterplot`; choose `market_value` and `goals`.
6. Switch the selected-variable axis radio button and point out the swapped axis labels.
7. Test a mixed view such as `position` and `goals`; mention categorical placement and jitter.
8. Hover over marks to show player, team, season, and values.
9. Mention the two source datasets, the join key, the final row/column counts, and the limitations.
